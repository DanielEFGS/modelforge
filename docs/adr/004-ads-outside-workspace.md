# ADR-004 — Advertising Outside the Interactive Workspace

## Status
Accepted.

## Decision
Advertising may surround or follow the ModelForge workspace but must not be embedded within its primary interaction panels or immediately adjacent to conversion/copy/download controls.

## Rationale
- preserve developer trust and repeat use,
- reduce accidental clicks,
- align with AdSense placement policy,
- protect editor usability and Core Web Vitals.

## Consequences
Revenue per page may be lower than an aggressive layout. The product deliberately optimizes for long-term repeat usage rather than maximum ad density.
